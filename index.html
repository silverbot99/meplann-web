How it works

Collect, plan, then go easy on yourself.

Board

Keep what you find

Share a YouTube video, a link, a photo, a Markdown note or a PDF straight from any app. It lands in My Day, ready to sort into a board.

Planner

Turn it into today

Collections, lists and tasks with steps and reminders. Pick a few for My Day and leave the rest where they are.

Gentle

Slow down when it's heavy

MePlann watches how full your plate is. When it gets heavy, it offers a minute of breathing and a smaller next step.

Just One Thing

Your whole list, one task at a time.

When My Day has open tasks, MePlann shows only the next one. Finish it or skip it, and the next one appears. The rest of the list waits out of sight, and your notes for today stay in a drawer below.

Workload score

A number for how full your plate is.

A 0–30 score from your open tasks, overdue items, unfinished steps and the time of day. Light, moderate or heavy. A ten-question check-in replaces it whenever the numbers miss how you actually feel.

0–9 light10–18 moderate19–30 heavy
AI companion

Someone to think out loud with.

Talk through a worry, reframe a stuck thought, break a task into steps, or pull a tarot card for a moment of reflection. The companion knows your work style and today's workload, so it doesn't push when you're already full.

Your data

Your plans stay on your phone.

Stored on your device

Boards, tasks, journal, chats and your self-portrait live on your iPhone. No account needed for boards and planner.

AI only when you ask

Text goes to OpenAI through our server only when you use an AI feature, and only after you agree. Our server doesn't keep your messages.

Encrypted backups, in your Drive

Optional backups go to your own Google Drive, encrypted with a key only you hold.

Recently Deleted

Deleted tasks, cards and chats wait 7 days before they're gone, so a slip of the thumb isn't final.
