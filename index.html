How it behaves

Practical when you need a plan,
gentle when you need a break

🫂

It learns how you work

A short self-discovery profile tells MePlann whether you think in structure or in bursts of ideas — and it plans differently depending on which one you are.

🧠

Reframes, not pep talks

When you're stuck or stressed, it doesn't just tell you to "push through." It breaks the problem into a smaller, actually doable next step.

🕊️

No guilt for resting

An unfinished list isn't treated as failure. If you're overloaded, MePlann quietly cuts down what it shows you instead of piling on more.

What makes it different

Built to fit you, not the other way around

Skip the system that never worked

You've probably tried Pomodoro, GTD, bullet journaling. MePlann doesn't ask you to adopt one more method — it adjusts its own suggestions to how your brain runs.

Room to think before you plan

A quick tarot pull or a five-minute brain dump, sitting right next to your task board — because clearing your head usually comes before clearing your list.

Your data stays yours

Everything is stored locally first. Cloud backup is there if you want it, never required — your unfiltered thoughts don't need to leave your device.

Why we're building this

Plan the day. Understand the pattern.

Standard productivity tools just push for maximum output. We’re asking a better question: why is a task effortless on Monday but completely overwhelming by Thursday? MePlann is built to help you discover those hidden patterns in your energy, one day at a time.
