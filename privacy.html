Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

MePlann keeps your data on your device. This policy explains what stays there, and the few cases where something leaves.

1. Data Collection & Usage

  • Personal Information: MePlann stores your profile, boards, tasks, notes, journal entries and chat history on your device, using Apple's Core Data and UserDefaults. There is no MePlann account and no server-side copy of this data.
  • Notifications: If you allow notifications, your device registers with Apple's push service. We do not send you push notifications from our servers; reminders are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Payments: Subscriptions are handled by Apple StoreKit. We never see your card details.

Two optional features send data off your device, and only when you turn them on: connecting a Google account (section 2) and the AI features (section 3).

2. Google User Data Policy

MePlann's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Connecting a Google account is entirely optional. MePlann works fully without it.

  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): Write-only. When you tap "Add to Calendar" on a task, MePlann creates an event on your primary calendar. MePlann cannot read your calendar — it never fetches, lists, or displays your existing events.
  • Google Drive (drive.file): Used for optional backup. When you tap "Back up now", MePlann writes a single file, meplann_backup.json, to your own Drive. It contains your boards, tasks, notes, chat history, braindumps and attached images. If you have connected Drive, images you attach to cards are also uploaded so they sync between your devices. This scope only lets MePlann see files it created itself — it cannot read the rest of your Drive.
  • Data Sharing: We do not share Google user data with any third party. It is never sold or used for advertising.
  • Data Storage & Protection: Google user data stays between your device and your own Google account. We do not store your Google data on our servers.
  • Data Retention & Deletion: Your backup file lives in your Drive and stays there until you delete it — signing out of MePlann does not remove it. You can revoke MePlann's access at any time in your Google account permissions, and delete the backup file directly from your Drive.

3. AI & Data Processing

  • AI Features: MePlann sends data to the OpenAI API for: chat, tarot readings, reframes, BrainFlow analysis, task brainstorming, task prioritization, smart task detection, feed card generation, and the wording of your reminder notifications.
  • Data Transmission: Your text input is sent, together with context that helps the assistant respond usefully: your work-style profile, your Multiple Intelligences results, your workload score, the current conversation history, and — when you @-mention a board or a date — the tasks and cards you referenced.
  • Requests you didn't start: To word your scheduled reminders, MePlann makes a small number of AI requests in the background, which include your workload score and how many tasks are pending or overdue. You can stop these by turning off notifications for MePlann.
  • Privacy: Per OpenAI's API data usage policy, data sent through the API is not used to train their models; OpenAI may retain it for a limited period for abuse monitoring. Your conversations are stored on your device, not on MePlann's servers.

4. Deleting your data

Because your data lives on your device, deleting the app deletes all of it — there is no account to close and nothing of yours left on our side.

If you connected Google: revoke MePlann's access in your Google account permissions, and delete meplann_backup.json from your Drive if you made a backup. Calendar events MePlann created stay in your calendar until you remove them.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions, contact us at support@meplann.com.
